# Chicken Soup with Ricotta Gnocchi

> Creamy, cheesy, cozy — the soup you'll crave all fall.

## Details

| | |
|-----|-----|
| **Cuisine** | Italian-American |
| **Servings** | 8 |

## Ingredients

### Ricotta Gnocchi
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Whole milk ricotta, drained | 1 cup | 250 g |
| Large egg | 1 | 1 |
| Finely grated Parmesan | ½ cup | 50 g |
| All-purpose flour, plus more for dusting | ¾ cup | 95 g |
| Kosher salt | ½ tsp | 3 g |
| Nutmeg | pinch | pinch |

### Soup
| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Drained julienne sun-dried tomatoes in oil | ½ cup | 85 g |
| Sun-dried tomato oil (from the jar) | 1 tbsp | 15 ml |
| Yellow onion, chopped | 2 cups | 284 g |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 4 (4 tsp) | 12 g |
| Tomato paste | 3 tbsp | 45 g |
| Chicken broth | 2 cartons (32 oz each) | 1.9 L |
| Heavy cream | 1 cup | 240 ml |

| Garlic powder | 1 tsp | 3 g |
| Crushed red pepper (+ more to garnish) | ¼ tsp | 1 g |
| Baby spinach, chopped | 5 oz | 142 g |
| Rotisserie chicken, shredded | 1¼ lb | 570 g |
| Mascarpone, room temp | 8 oz | 227 g |
| Parmesan, shredded (+ extra to garnish) | 6 oz | 165 g |

## Instructions

### Make the Gnocchi
1. Mix ricotta, egg, Parmesan, salt, and nutmeg in a bowl until combined.
2. Gently fold in flour until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms. Don't overmix.
3. On a floured surface, divide dough into 4 pieces. Roll each into a ¾-inch rope and cut into 1-inch pieces.
4. Boil in salted water until they float, about 2–3 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside.

### Make the Soup
1. Heat 1 tbsp sun-dried tomato oil in a Dutch oven over medium. Sauté onion and garlic 3–4 minutes.
2. Stir in tomato paste and sun-dried tomatoes, cook 2 minutes.
3. Add broth, cream, basil, salt, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, and red pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce to low.
4. Stir in spinach, chicken, mascarpone, and Parmesan. Cook 3 minutes until creamy and heated through.
5. Gently fold in the cooked ricotta gnocchi and cook 1–2 minutes more.

### Serve
1. Garnish with extra Parmesan, basil, and red pepper. Serve hot.

## Notes

- Drain the ricotta well for firmer gnocchi — press it through cheesecloth or let it sit in a fine-mesh strainer for 30 minutes.
- The gnocchi can be made ahead and refrigerated on a floured sheet pan for up to a day.
