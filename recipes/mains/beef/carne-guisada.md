# Carne Guisada

> Slow-simmered beef in a rich, savory gravy — pure comfort food full of deep flavor and tradition. Serve with warm tortillas for that unforgettable, soul-satisfying bite.

## Details

| | |
|-----|-----|
| **Cuisine** | Mexican / Tex-Mex |
| **Servings** | 6–8 |
| **Prep Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 1.5–2 hours |
| **Total Time** | 1.75–2.25 hours |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Beef stew meat, cut into chunks | 3 lb | 1.4 kg |
| Ground cumin | 2 tsp | 4 g |
| Black pepper | 1/2 tsp | 1 g |
| Tomato sauce (or 1 diced fresh tomato) | 3 tbsp | 45 ml |
| Garlic cloves, minced | 3 | 3 |
| Salt | 2 tsp | 12 g |
| All-purpose flour | 2 tbsp | 16 g |
| Chicken broth or water | 1 cup | 240 ml |
| Oil or lard | 1 tbsp | 15 ml |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 | 1 |
| Small green bell pepper, diced (optional) | 1 | 1 |

## Instructions

### Brown the Meat
1. Heat the oil or lard in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef chunks, diced onion, and bell pepper if using. Cook until the meat develops a deep brown crust and the vegetables soften.

### Build the Flavor
1. Stir in the garlic, tomato sauce (or fresh tomato), cumin, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 2–3 minutes until everything smells rich and fragrant.

### Thicken the Gravy
1. Sprinkle the flour over the meat and stir well to coat evenly. This helps create a thick, silky gravy.

### Simmer Low and Slow
1. Slowly pour in the broth while stirring to avoid lumps. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low.
2. Cover and simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours, or until the beef is fork-tender and the sauce has thickened. Stir occasionally.

### Serve
1. Serve with warm flour tortillas.

## Notes

- For a thicker gravy, simmer uncovered for the last 20–30 minutes.
- Leftovers reheat well and taste even better the next day.
