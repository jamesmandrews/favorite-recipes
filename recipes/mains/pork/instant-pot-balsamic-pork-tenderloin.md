# Instant Pot Balsamic Pork Tenderloin

Ultra tender pork tenderloin with a balsamic glaze made in the pressure cooker.

- **Source:** Tiffany Azure
- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 35 minutes
- **Total Time:** 50 minutes
- **Servings:** 4-6

## Ingredients

- 1-2 pork tenderloins (1-1.5 lbs / 450-680 g each, see notes)
- 2 tablespoons (30 ml) olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons dried Italian herb blend (see notes)
- 4 tablespoons (60 ml) soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons (40 g) brown sugar (see notes)
- 1/2 cup (120 ml) balsamic vinegar
- 1 cup (240 ml) water
- 2 tablespoons (16 g) corn starch + 4 tablespoons (60 ml) cold water

## Instructions

1. Set pressure cooker to **SAUTE**. Rub tenderloins all over with oil. Season generously on all sides with salt, pepper, dried herbs, and garlic powder.
2. Use tongs to place tenderloins in the pot, turning every 1-2 minutes until browned on all sides.
3. Whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, balsamic vinegar, and water. Add to the pot. Lock the lid with the steam valve closed. Set to **PRESSURE COOK** or **MANUAL** for 15 minutes.
4. When cook time is up, allow to natural release for 5 minutes, then turn the steam valve to the venting position. Remove lid once the float valve drops down. Transfer pork to a cutting board and set aside.
5. Switch pressure cooker to **SOUP** setting. Once boiling, stir corn starch into the 4 tablespoons cold water until dissolved, then pour into the pot and stir until thickened to a glaze.
6. Slice tenderloins and serve with balsamic glaze on top. Goes great over mashed potatoes.

## Notes

- One large or two small-medium pork tenderloins (1-1.5 lbs / 450-680 g each) work perfectly. One small-medium tenderloin will leave extra glaze.
- In place of the dried herb blend, substitute 1 teaspoon dried thyme + 1 teaspoon dried basil + 1/4 teaspoon dried rosemary.
- Original recipe calls for 1 cup brown sugar — way too sweet. 3 tablespoons is plenty.
- Extra glaze keeps in the fridge up to 2 weeks, or freeze in ice cube trays for longer storage.
