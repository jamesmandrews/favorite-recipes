# Instant Pot Balsamic Pork Tenderloin

> Ultra tender pork tenderloin with a balsamic glaze made in the pressure cooker.

## Details

| | |
|-----|-----|
| **Source** | Tiffany Azure |
| **Cuisine** | American |
| **Servings** | 4-6 |
| **Prep Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 35 minutes |
| **Total Time** | 50 minutes |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Imperial | Metric |
|------------|----------|--------|
| Pork tenderloins (see notes) | 1-2 (1-1.5 lb each) | 1-2 (450-680 g each) |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp | 30 ml |
| Salt and pepper | to taste | to taste |
| Garlic powder | 1 tsp | 3 g |
| Dried Italian herb blend (see notes) | 2 tsp | 4 g |
| Soy sauce | 4 tbsp | 60 ml |
| Brown sugar (see notes) | 3 tbsp | 40 g |
| Balsamic vinegar | ½ cup | 120 ml |
| Water | 1 cup | 240 ml |
| Corn starch | 2 tbsp | 16 g |
| Cold water (for slurry) | 4 tbsp | 60 ml |

## Instructions

### Sear
1. Set pressure cooker to **SAUTE**. Rub tenderloins all over with oil. Season generously on all sides with salt, pepper, dried herbs, and garlic powder.
2. Use tongs to place tenderloins in the pot, turning every 1-2 minutes until browned on all sides.

### Pressure Cook
1. Whisk together soy sauce, brown sugar, balsamic vinegar, and water. Add to the pot. Lock the lid with the steam valve closed. Set to **PRESSURE COOK** or **MANUAL** for 15 minutes.
2. When cook time is up, allow to natural release for 5 minutes, then turn the steam valve to the venting position. Remove lid once the float valve drops down. Transfer pork to a cutting board and set aside.

### Make the Glaze
1. Switch pressure cooker to **SOUP** setting. Once boiling, stir corn starch into the 4 tablespoons cold water until dissolved, then pour into the pot and stir until thickened to a glaze.

### Serve
1. Slice tenderloins and serve with balsamic glaze on top. Goes great over mashed potatoes.

## Notes

- One large or two small-medium pork tenderloins (1-1.5 lb / 450-680 g each) work perfectly. One small-medium tenderloin will leave extra glaze.
- In place of the dried herb blend, substitute 1 tsp dried thyme + 1 tsp dried basil + ¼ tsp dried rosemary.
- Original recipe calls for 1 cup brown sugar — way too sweet. 3 tablespoons is plenty.
- Extra glaze keeps in the fridge up to 2 weeks, or freeze in ice cube trays for longer storage.
